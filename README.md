## 🤔 About Me
Hi, I'm Wagner! 👋

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Informatics and Computing Engineering and a Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto (FEUP).

My Master's thesis, **"Multi-Agent Framework for Automated Reproduction of UAV Positioning Solutions from Research Papers"**, was awarded **18/20**. In this work, I developed **MARS**, a multi-agent framework that leverages Large Language Models to automate the reproduction of scientific papers into traceable, executable, and validated implementations.

I'm passionate about Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Backend Development, Distributed Systems, and Software Engineering. I enjoy building scalable software, exploring AI-driven solutions, and continuously learning new technologies.
